The plan the household set at the start of the month, against what actually left the account. Change a plan and the bars move at once.

How the plan is set

Fixed costs first — rent, insurance, after-school care — then groceries by the weekly shop, then the envelopes the household can actually control: dining out, entertainment, shopping. A category that runs over two months in a row gets its plan raised instead of its spending scolded; see the reports for the six-month picture.
