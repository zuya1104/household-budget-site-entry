The rule behind the goals

Whatever is left at the end of the month is split by the same rule every time: half to the emergency fund until it is full, then a quarter each to the vacation and the car. The emergency fund comes first because a broken car or a lost week's pay should never become debt — see reports for how fast the savings are actually growing.
